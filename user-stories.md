# User Stories — Patient Appointment & Scheduling System

## US-01 — Book a New Appointment
**As a** patient
**I want** to book a new appointment with an available provider
**So that** I can receive care at a time that fits my schedule

- **Priority:** High
- **Acceptance Criteria:** See `Feature: Appointment Booking`

## US-02 — Reschedule an Existing Appointment
**As a** patient
**I want** to reschedule an existing appointment to a new date/time
**So that** I can adjust my visit without losing my place in the system

- **Priority:** High
- **Acceptance Criteria:** See `Feature: Appointment Rescheduling`

## US-03 — Cancel an Appointment
**As a** patient
**I want** to cancel an appointment I no longer need
**So that** the slot can be freed up for another patient

- **Priority:** High
- **Acceptance Criteria:** See `Feature: Appointment Cancellation`

## US-04 — Receive Appointment Reminders
**As a** patient
**I want** to receive a reminder before my appointment
**So that** I don't forget or arrive at the wrong time

- **Priority:** Medium
- **Acceptance Criteria:** See `Feature: Appointment Reminders`

## US-05 — Prevent Double-Booking
**As a** healthcare provider
**I want** the system to reject overlapping bookings against my calendar
**So that** I am never scheduled for two patients at once

- **Priority:** High
- **Acceptance Criteria:** See `Feature: Appointment Booking`

## US-06 — Staff-Assisted Booking
**As a** clinic staff member
**I want** to book, reschedule, or cancel appointments on behalf of a patient
**So that** I can support patients who call or walk in instead of using the app

- **Priority:** Medium
- **Acceptance Criteria:** See `Feature: Appointment Booking`

## US-07 — View Personal Appointment History
**As a** patient
**I want** to view my past and upcoming appointments in one place
**So that** I can track my care history without contacting the clinic

- **Priority:** Low
- **Acceptance Criteria:** See `Feature: Appointment History`
